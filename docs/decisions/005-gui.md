# 005: GUI

Status: accepted (2026-10-13)

## Context

The GUI ([002](002-technology-stack.md)) is one React app for the Desktop, browsers and later the phone. It shows many kinds of content side by side: the Daemon, Events, later Repositories, Terminals, Agents and files. How content is named, placed and kept is costly to change once there are many Views. How the Server serves the GUI, the token and the Content-Security-Policy are in [004](004-server.md).

## Decision

- **Terms:** a View shows something, by its kind and parameters; a Panel is a tab showing one View; Groups hold Panels as tabs, side by side or stacked; the Layout arranges the Groups ([glossary](../glossary.md)). dockview (`dockview-react`) provides Panels and Groups.
- **Views know nothing of dockview:** `gui/web/src/views/` has a folder per kind, named with its icon in `views/kinds.ts` and its component in `views/index.ts`; a View gets its parameters as props, with ways to change them and its tab's title. Only `layout/` uses dockview, so that a phone shell can show the same Views as pages, and dockview's frequent major versions stay in one place. Views open other Views through `useOpenView` (`views/opening.ts`), which `layout/` provides with our `openView`, never dockview's API: from a Panel's Group from within it, elsewhere from the active Group (`layout/ViewOpener.tsx`), e.g. from the status bar, whose Daemon icon, coloured by the Daemon's health, opens the Daemon View.
- **Addresses:** a View's address is its kind and parameters as text, sorted: `events?repository=…`; a parameter with several values repeats, `kind=clone&kind=worktree`. A Panel keeps its View's kind and parameters in dockview's Panel parameters, which a View can change, e.g. by filtering, and are saved with the Layout; its ID is random, as a Panel's View can change. Opening a View that a Panel shows (the same address) focuses that Panel, its tab flashing in the accent for a moment to show where it is; two Panels may show the same View. The phone shell will use addresses as page URLs, after `#`.
- **Opening:** "+" in each Group's tab bar lists the kinds; an empty Group lists the suggested ones; a View opens others as previews, e.g. by a `ViewLink`, a button with the kind's icon (and its name, where there's room; a View's bar ends in icons alone), greyed out where it would show nothing that needs a look, e.g. a Checkout without changes: by a click in its own Group, by the middle button or Alt+click beside it, as browsers open a link in place or in a new tab (Ctrl and Shift stay free for selecting in lists). Beside is the Group touching it on the right, else below, left or above; of several on that side, the topmost (right, left) or leftmost (below, above), by their sizes on screen (`layout/beside.ts`); a Group alone is split, to the right. A preview is one per Group, which the next preview replaces, also the one that opened it (the Panel stays, its View changes), its title in italics; it is kept, an ordinary Panel, once its tab is double-clicked (not a link: its second click would land in the View opened), it is changed (filtered, sorted, …), or it is dragged to another Group. Previews are a Panel parameter beside its View's, so the Layout keeps them. Later also from sidebars (dockview's edge groups) and a command palette.
- **Saving:** the Layout is kept per browser, in localStorage, with a format number. A Panel whose kind no longer exists says so; a View that fails, e.g. on parameters it no longer understands, shows its error in its Panel only; a Layout that can't be restored gives the default one. No migrations.
- **Keys:** Ctrl+W closes the active Panel (or empty Group); only the Desktop passes it to the page, as browsers close their tab. Ctrl+F goes to the active Panel's search field, if its View has one; else the browser finds in the page. Each Group goes back and forward through the Views it showed, as a browser tab does (`layout/visits.ts`): by the mouse's side buttons in the Group under the pointer, or Alt+←/→ in the active one, which the page takes from the browser. Each address a Group shows is a step: another Panel's, or another in the same Panel (a link replacing a preview, a filter, another Revision compared), but keys typed in a row, e.g. a search, are one. Going back shows a View again in its Panel, or as a preview where the Panel is gone. The steps are kept until the page is left.
- **Empty Groups:** a Group stays when its last Panel closes or moves away (dockview's `closeEmptyGroups: false`), showing the suggested Views, so a Layout keeps its shape; an empty Group, but not the last, offers to close, as does Ctrl+W.
- **Later, Workspaces:** one window switches between Workspaces, ordered by hand (lists of Repositories are ordered by name or activity instead), e.g. one per Repository, each with its own Panels in a named Layout that Workspaces share (at first, all the same one). Each Workspace keeps its own dockview, all of them mounted and the hidden ones not asking again until shown; a shared Layout that changed is applied when its Workspace is shown. This needs Groups that stay when empty.
- **Live updates:** the GUI asks the Server again only when its live connection hints at a change, never on a timer (`api/live.ts`, which maps each hint to the queries it covers, by the names in `KEYS`); connecting asks again for everything, and gaining focus asks for a pass and for what changes by hand: the settings, and Checkouts' changes, as editing a file changes no State. Answers stay current until then (`staleTime: Infinity`). It reconnects after 1, 2, 4 … 30 s, and at once on focus; 25 s without a heartbeat count as lost. The status bar says live or since when disconnected, the Daemon's icon turns lost, and the Daemon View says what to check.
- **Lists:** TanStack Table (`@tanstack/react-table`, headless) holds a list's columns and their state; Views render them as CSS grids of their own markup (`components/table/`). A row opens its details by the button in its first cell, or by a click anywhere else on it but on its cells' own buttons and links, and not one selecting text. Tooltips are native (`title`): a cell's own where it adds something (a full time, a status spelled out), else its whole text while cut off; a header's says what the column shows, if its label doesn't. Columns may sort (TanStack's sorting, off unless a column turns it on; the order is a View parameter, `sort`), rows may hold rows (`getSubRows`, `data-depth`), which rows that fold open instead of details (the Changes View's groups, directories with changes, and new folders, whose files are asked for once opened), rows that open another View instead, as a link does (a changed file its Diff), marked while that View is shown in any Group, focused or not, as VS Code marks the file of the editor shown (`useIsShown`, from `layout/`; also a commit in History while its Changes are), and rows that show all they have open nothing (a note), and a filter may be linked: set only from outside, e.g. by a link, and offered only while set. Narrow Panels show a row in two lines: its square and icons beside what it is, its details below, one after the other rather than in columns, which would only line up by chance, and times at the end, where they line up. Column widths can be dragged; they are kept per kind of View in the browser (`hephaistos.columns.<kind>`), shared by all its Panels, and not part of addresses. Widths reach the grid as CSS variables. A column's header filters by it: one choice for a point on a scale (a priority or higher, since a time), any of several for a set (kinds, Repositories), none meaning all. Filters are the View's parameters, by the names of the API's, which filters like the CLI; so they are saved with the Layout, and the tab's title names them.
- **Controls:** React Aria Components (`react-aria-components`, unstyled), styled with our tokens, for controls beyond the browser's own: a search field (`components/SearchField`), its text in the View's parameters at each key, asked with once typing pauses (`useDebounced`) or on Enter; a Revision picker (`components/GitRevisionPicker`): fixed rows (working tree, index, HEAD) and tabs of branches, tags and commits, as each use chooses, after All, a few of each with a row leading to their tab; typing shows All, filtered (refs in the browser, ranked: the same name, starting with it, at a word's start, within; commits searched by the Server, from HEAD or everywhere), an exact name or a hash's commit on top; a kind's tab lists all of it (virtualized, commits loading more on scrolling); Enter picks the first row, and with none resolves the text as git does; Ctrl+←/→ between tabs. Refs are named by their full names (`refs/heads/main`), never mistaken for another ref or a Revision, and shown short.
- **Diffs:** the Diff View shows git's patch of one file (`diff?clone=…&from=…&to=…&path=…`) with `gui.diff.context` lines around each change, and fills it in with the file's two versions, asked for after it, so that it says how many lines it leaves out up to the file's end, and shows them on asking; rendered by `@pierre/diffs`, pinned, imported only by `components/diff/` and loaded once a diff shows, so another renderer could replace it; Shiki highlights it with the themes `gui.code.theme_light` and `gui.code.theme_dark` name, on the page's background, in Web Workers (a diff shows as plain text until its colours come); its header is ours, as the file's row in Changes shows it, and stays in view below the View's bar while its diff does; the lines left out between changes show as a quiet band between two lines, not the renderer's bars, so that where a file starts stands out. Without `path`, the Diff View shows all files of the comparison or commit one under the other, in the order Changes lists them (Changes links to it at its bar's end): each as high as its lines are likely to be until it comes near the scrolled area, then asked for, shown with its versions (asked for again, e.g. on focus or a live hint, a new patch shows once its versions came too, and unchanged ones leave it as it is), and held as high as its patch's rows and bands make it until drawn, so that it changes height once, if at all (`components/diff/height.ts`), the renderer's `Virtualizer` drawing only the lines in view; files under their directories' headers (nested, in their parents', as Changes nests them), which stay in view above the file's header and line up with it; a click on a file's or directory's header collapses it to its header, and another shows it again (a directory's files as they were), while that comparison shows; a choice in the bar collapses all files at once, leaving their headers as an index, or shows all again (neither marked while mixed); untracked files and new folders, which may be anything left lying about, last, listed as Changes lists them, each file's diff shown below its row once opened, or all at once. Side by side (inline in narrow Panels; a new or deleted file's lines on their own side, as other files' are) or inline, wrapped or scrolled, the whole file or its changes (turned off, the diff starts anew, as the renderer can't fold lines shown again): chosen for all Diffs, kept per browser (`hephaistos.diff`). Large diffs (`gui.diff.large_lines`) wait to be shown; an empty one offers where the file's changes are now, in the same Panel.
- **Comparisons:** the Changes and Diff Views show first in their bar what they compare: two Revisions picked (`from` ⇄ `to`, swapped by a button between), or for a commit (`commit=`) its line in their place, opening all about it below the bar, with a button to pick other Revisions instead, from its first parent; without `from` and `to`, Changes shows HEAD → working tree, in `git status`'s groups or together, a choice at the bar's end offered only for that pair and kept per browser (`hephaistos.changes`), as the Diffs' look is. Directories show each by its path or nested in their parents (one holding a single directory and nothing else joined with it, `src/hephaistos/`), a choice in both Views' bars (in the Diff's only for all files, whose order it sets), kept per browser with the other. Below, what the comparison holds, its files and lines, is the first row of the list or the stack, its lines lined up with the rows' and headers', scrolling with them; a Diff's file header names the file and its lines, not the comparison again.
- **History:** the History View lists a Checkout's commits as `git log` walks back (`history?clone=…`), from HEAD or a branch, tag or commit picked (`ref`, HEAD as none, following what is checked out), of all files or one path (`path`), a file's renames marked where they happened, and only commits whose message has all words searched for (`search`, in any order and case), or, for a hash, its commit first, else where it is: on other branches, offered to show it there, or not changing the path, offered with all files; a row opens all about its commit (the commit box, also under a commit's Changes and in a commit Event's details); while HEAD's commits show, a row above them says what isn't committed, linking to the Changes. A commit hash shown on its own is a link to the commit's changes (`changes?clone=…&commit=…`, against its first parent), which say if no branch or tag leads to the commit any more, and what amends of it led to. History opens from a Checkout's row in Repositories, and from the bars of its Changes and of a file's Diff.
- **Later, other Machines' logs:** logs aren't synced, so another Machine's log comes from that Machine's Server, once the GUI reaches other Servers (decided with remote Machines). The Log View takes the Machine as a parameter, `log?machine=…`, this Machine without one, chosen above the list rather than filtered: a log is one Machine's at a time.
- **Look:** CSS modules and the custom properties of `theme.css`, with VS Code's greys; dockview's light theme for sizes, with its colours from `theme.css` (`layout/dock.css`), so it follows the system's light or dark. Monochrome icons from Lucide (`lucide-react`), mapped in `icons.tsx` by action and entity type, a dot for anything else. Icons beside text sit a little above the middle of its capitals, by two values in `theme.css` (`--icon-drop` inline, `--icon-lift` when centred), not by nudges per icon. Monospace among text is a little smaller (`--mono-size`), as its letters look larger. What Views show takes its size and font from `[gui.text]`, code from `[gui.code]` (`look.ts`); tabs, View bars and the status bar keep theirs, so zooming isn't needed to read. Tabs are at most 15rem wide, a longer title cut off with its whole text as a tooltip. A choice between two ways of showing, e.g. side by side or inline, is a `Choice` in the View's bar: both ways' icons, the one taken marked, each with its tooltip, a click taking the other. Lines between Groups and between a list's groups of rows have their own colours (`--divider`, `--line`). Brand logos only for links to forges (`forges.ts`), from Simple Icons (`simple-icons`, CC0, imported one by one). Each kind of View has one, shown before its tab's title (`layout/ViewTab.tsx`, around dockview's own tab) and in the lists of kinds.

## Rejected

- A Layout per Repository: rearranging one wouldn't rearrange the others, as with separate VS Code windows.
- Swapping Panels when switching Workspaces: it would recreate every View, losing Terminals' state and scroll positions.
- Layouts in the Registry: an arrangement fits a screen, not every device.
